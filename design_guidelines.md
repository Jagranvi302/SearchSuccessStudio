# Portfolio Website Design Guidelines

## Design Approach
**Reference-Based Approach**: Drawing inspiration from modern professional portfolios like those found on Behance, Dribbble, and personal sites of established designers/marketers. The design should convey expertise, credibility, and modern web standards.

## Core Design Elements

### A. Color Palette
**Primary Colors:**
- Dark mode: Deep navy (220 25% 12%) as primary background
- Light mode: Clean white (0 0% 98%) as primary background
- Accent: Professional blue (215 80% 55%) for CTAs and highlights
- Text: High contrast grays (220 15% 85% for dark mode, 220 15% 20% for light mode)

### B. Typography
- **Primary**: Inter or Poppins from Google Fonts for modern, professional appeal
- **Headings**: Bold weights (600-700) for strong hierarchy
- **Body**: Regular (400) and medium (500) weights for readability
- **Scale**: Large hero text (3xl-5xl), clear section headings (xl-2xl), readable body (base-lg)

### C. Layout System
- **Spacing**: Consistent use of Tailwind units 4, 8, 12, 16, and 24 for predictable rhythm
- **Grid**: 12-column responsive grid with generous gutters
- **Containers**: Max-width constraints (max-w-6xl) for optimal reading lengths
- **Sections**: Clear separation with ample whitespace between content blocks

### D. Component Library
**Navigation**: Fixed/sticky header with smooth scroll navigation and mobile hamburger menu
**Hero Section**: Large typography with professional headshot, title, and primary CTA
**Cards**: Clean service/skill cards with subtle shadows and hover states
**Timeline**: Professional experience displayed in vertical timeline format
**Contact**: Simple form with input styling matching overall aesthetic
**Buttons**: Primary (filled) and secondary (outline) variants with professional blue accent

### E. Visual Treatments
**Gradients**: Subtle background gradients using blues and grays (220 25% 15% to 220 20% 10%) for depth
**Shadows**: Soft, modern drop shadows for card elements and sections
**Borders**: Minimal use of subtle borders (1px) in muted grays for separation

## Images
**Professional Headshot**: High-quality portrait photo in the hero section, positioned prominently alongside the introduction text. Should be well-lit, professional attire, and convey approachability.

**Background Elements**: Optional subtle pattern overlays or geometric shapes to add visual interest without distraction.

**Icons**: Use Heroicons for consistent iconography throughout (services, skills, contact methods).

**No large hero background image** - focus remains on professional headshot and typography for maximum impact and fast loading.

## Key Principles
- **Professional Credibility**: Clean, modern design that builds trust
- **Scannable Content**: Clear hierarchy and organized sections for easy browsing
- **Mobile-First**: Responsive design optimized for all devices
- **Performance**: Fast loading with optimized assets and minimal animations
- **SEO-Optimized**: Semantic HTML structure with proper heading hierarchy
